# Introduction notes

The introduction presents the shared clustering project, motivation, goal, and objectives; detailed formulations, source comparisons, and experimental protocols belong in later sections. The QAOA [[1]](ref.md#farhi2014-qaoa) and quantum PCA [[2]](ref.md#lloyd2014-qpca) papers provide foundational context, not evidence of project performance.

Open design questions include the quantum PCA method and classical readout, graph construction and balance settings, optimizer candidates, noise parameters, and application resource limits. The additional semi-supervised classification experiment remains separate, with its method and evaluation design unconfirmed. Do not present planned functionality or results as completed during assembly.
